Address Autocomplete for Gravity Forms

1.0.0
-----
* One-time administrator setup notice with permanent dismissal and no activation redirect.
* Initial standalone release.
* Google Places API (New) autocomplete with a temporary legacy compatibility fallback.
* Dedicated single-line Address field with an entry-detail Google Maps link.
* Autocomplete and address-component autofill for native Gravity Forms Address fields.
* Per-field country, place-type, language and location bias preferences, plus global language and region settings.
* Optional required suggestion selection with a custom clear-and-alert message on blur and submission checks.
* Autocomplete support for AJAX and conditionally displayed fields and entry editing.
* Local product overview, package comparison, help and other plugin information.
